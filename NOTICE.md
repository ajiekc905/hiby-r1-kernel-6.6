## Data taken from the stock firmware

Three tables are byte sequences read from the device's stock firmware
rather than values re-derived from datasheets: the two panel initialisation
sequences in `panel-hiby-r1.c`, the 64-byte CW2015 battery profile in
`hiby_r1.dts`, and the PMIC start-up register writes in `axp2101-r1.c`.
They are hardware configuration data needed for the device to work, and the
kernel they came from is itself GPL-2.0. The source of the stock kernel
modules was not available; the drivers here were written from the
observed register behaviour of the device.

## If you distribute a built kernel

A built `vmlinux` or `xImage` is a GPL-2.0 binary. It also embeds a static
BusyBox (GPL-2.0) and, with `PROD=1`, `r1screen` linked against musl (MIT).
Whoever distributes it must offer the corresponding source: the SDK commit
above, this tree at the revision used, the BusyBox version and
`initramfs/busybox.config`.

Do not distribute a complete `.upt` update image: it carries the vendor's
root filesystem. The Wi-Fi and Bluetooth firmware files are likewise not
included; the init scripts load them from the stock root filesystem that
is already on the device.
